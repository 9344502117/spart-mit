About SPART

SPART – Society of Plastics and Rubber Technologists is a student association at Madras Institute of Technology (MIT), Anna University, dedicated to the field of plastics and rubber technology.

SPART represents a platform for students to connect with their academic interests, explore the world of polymer science and technology, and engage with the evolving plastics and rubber industry.

Through its association activities, technical initiatives, and events, SPART aims to create an engaging environment that encourages learning, creativity, collaboration, and student participation.

More than just an association, SPART is a community that brings together curiosity, knowledge, and a shared passion for materials and technology.
